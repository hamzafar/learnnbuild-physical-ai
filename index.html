About

While building an end-to-end robotics perception stack, I realized that learning Physical AI often requires piecing together information from many different sources. This initiative was created to bring those concepts together through practical, project-based learning.

The goal is to create free learning resources and open-source projects that help engineers, students, and researchers learn by building complete Physical AI systems step by step.

The projects are developed openly on GitHub and continuously updated as new capabilities are added.

  • autonomous_perception End-to-end perception stack
  • spatial_perception Spatial reasoning & mapping
